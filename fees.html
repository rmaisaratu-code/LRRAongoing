Fees

Clear, personal and straight from the school office.

Request the fee schedule

Tuition differs by level and is payable per term. Tell us which level you are interested in and the school office will send you the full schedule, payment terms and any available instalment arrangement.

How we use your information. The details you give here are used only by the school to respond to you, process your request or consider your application. They are shared only with staff who need them, kept securely, and never sold. Information about children is handled with particular care. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.

What your fees sustain

Fees keep the things that make the school work: qualified teachers, small classes, careful supervision, health and safety provisions, a counselling office and a structured programme of learning from Crèche to SS 3. They are an investment in your child’s knowledge, character and future options, and we work hard to make sure every family feels that value.
